ABOUT US

Art That Tells Your Story

Chukwusom Pencil Art is a creative art brand based in Nnewi, Anambra State, specializing in detailed pencil portraits and artistic drawings.

Every artwork is carefully created to capture the personality, emotion and beauty of the subject. Whether it is a gift for someone special, a family portrait or a beautiful piece for your home, we turn memorable moments into timeless artwork.
